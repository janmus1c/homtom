ROOT CAUSE of the user-mode hang (IMG_3670): "cr_alignment 4c8000d4 live cr c000717f". 0x4C8000D4 = LCD WIN1MAP address, left in r0 by the early colour-ladder
HOMTOM_PROBE_POST (6g_reveal) inserted right after the MMU-enable mcr in head.S (macro clobbers r0,r1). r0 (= CP15 control value) is saved by __mmap_switched as cr_alignment;
usr_entry/vector_swi alignment_trap rewrites CP15 c1 from cr_alignment on every user-mode exception => MMU bit cleared => instant death at the first user-mode exception.
(Likely also why the bootloader machine ID read as 0 earlier: r1 was clobbered by the same macro.) Fix: PROBE_POST removed; ALIGNMENT_TRAP back on; FIQ sampler not armed;
/init probe execs busybox sh /init.sh (HELLO_EXEC).
